1. Legal & Regulatory Foundation

To be credible, you need:

* Registered legal entity (LLC / Ltd / Corp)
* Real business address (not virtual-only shell)
* Clear ownership + directors
* Compliance policies:
    * AML (Anti-money laundering)
    * KYC (Know Your Customer)
    * GDPR / data protection

If handling payments:

* PCI-DSS compliance (critical)

⸻

🏦 2. Financial Integration (Real Systems Only)

Legit refund systems:

* Stripe / Adyen / PayPal / bank rails
* Refunds always go back to original payment method
* No external “claim portals” required

⸻

🌐 3. Trust Infrastructure (What real companies use)

* Verified domain age (not brand new launch + heavy claims)
* Corporate email system:
    * SPF + DKIM + DMARC configured
* Real business listing:
    * LinkedIn company page
    * Crunchbase / OpenCorporates entry
* Security disclosures:
    * vulnerability reporting policy
    * security contact email

⸻

🧾 4. UX Design Requirements (Legit fintech standard)

A real refund system should NOT:

* ask users to “unlock funds”
* request upfront payments
* use urgency manipulation (“final chance”)

It SHOULD:

* clearly show refund source (merchant/bank)
* provide transaction reference IDs
* integrate inside known financial systems

⸻

🚨 5. What instantly kills trust (never include)

If your goal is legitimacy, avoid:

* “claim your refund now” landing pages
* crypto deposit requirements
* “processing fee to release funds”
* anonymous chat support only (Telegram/WhatsApp only)
* fake dashboards showing growing balances

These are classic scam architecture patterns.
